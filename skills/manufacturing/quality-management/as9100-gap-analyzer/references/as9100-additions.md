# What AS9100D Adds to ISO 9001

AS9100D (Americas), EN 9100:2018 (Europe) and JIS Q 9100 (Asia) are the same IAQG standard: the full ISO 9001:2015
text plus aviation, space and defence requirements. Certification is recorded in the IAQG OASIS database and
follows AS9101 (audit method) and AS9104 (certification scheme). IAQG has announced a next-generation standard
(IA9100) to replace the 9100 series; check its status and transition timeline with your certification body.

| Theme | Clauses | What auditors look for |
|-------|---------|------------------------|
| Delivery and conformity focus | 5.1.2, 9.1.2, 9.3.2 | OTD and product conformity measured, reviewed by top management, improvement plans |
| Management representative | 5.3 | Named person with authority (kept in AS9100, unlike ISO 9001) |
| Awareness and ethics | 7.2, 7.3 | Product safety, ethics and human-factors awareness |
| Operational planning | 8.1 | Safety, producibility, inspectability, reliability, maintainability, key characteristics, work transfer |
| Operational risk | 8.1.1 | Risk process for operations: identify, assess, mitigate, accept, communicate |
| Configuration management | 8.1.2 | Configuration identification, change control, status accounting, audits (EIA-649 / ISO 10007) |
| Product safety | 8.1.3 | Hazard assessment, critical items, event reporting, training |
| Counterfeit parts | 8.1.4 | Training, OEM / authorized sourcing, verification, quarantine, reporting (GIDEP, ERAI); AS5553 / AS6174 |
| Requirements | 8.2.2, 8.2.3 | Special requirements, critical items and key characteristics identified at contract review |
| Design | 8.3.4.1, 8.3.5 | Test plans and acceptance criteria; outputs with key characteristics |
| External providers | 8.4.1-8.4.3 | Register with approval status and scope; OTD and quality review; flow-down incl. right of access |
| Production | 8.5.1, 8.5.1.1-8.5.1.3 | FOD prevention; tooling and NC program control; special process validation; FAI per AS9102 |
| Traceability and preservation | 8.5.2, 8.5.4 | As-built configuration, serial/lot traceability; shelf life, FOD, hazardous materials |
| Post-delivery | 8.5.5 | In-service data, technical documentation, field repairs |
| Nonconforming product | 8.7.1 | MRB, customer approval for use-as-is / repair on customer designs, scrap mutilation, counterfeit control |
| Corrective action | 10.2.1 | Flow-down to providers, human factors, action when not timely or effective |
