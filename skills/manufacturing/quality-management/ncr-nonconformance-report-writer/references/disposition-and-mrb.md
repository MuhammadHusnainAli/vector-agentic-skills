# Disposition and Material Review

## Disposition options

| Disposition | Meaning | Required before release |
|-------------|---------|-------------------------|
| Rework | Make the item conform to the original requirement (re-machine, re-test) | Approved rework instruction; re-inspection to the original requirement |
| Repair | Make it acceptable for use though still not conforming to the drawing (weld repair, sleeve) | Concession from the customer / design authority; re-inspection to the repair spec |
| Use-as-is | Accept the nonconformity unchanged | Concession from the customer / design authority; record the authority |
| Scrap | Destroy so it cannot be used | Mutilation or controlled scrap bin; record quantity |
| Return to supplier | Send back for credit, rework or replacement | Supplier notified; SCAR if systemic |
| Regrade | Use for another application where it meets requirements | Engineering approval; re-identify the part |

ISO 9001 8.7.1 allows correction, segregation/containment/return/suspension, informing the customer and
**acceptance under concession**; 8.7.2 requires records of the nonconformity, actions, concessions and the
authority deciding. IATF 16949 8.7.1.1 requires **customer authorization** before shipping product that deviates
(including repair and use-as-is) and 8.7.1.4-8.7.1.5 controls rework and repair with documented processes and
customer approval for repair. AS9100 8.7.1 prohibits use-as-is or repair dispositions by the organization when the
customer owns the design, unless the design authority authorizes it.

## Material review board (MRB)

| Role | Contributes |
|------|-------------|
| Quality engineer (chair) | Requirement, evidence, risk of escape |
| Manufacturing / process engineer | Rework feasibility, cause hypotheses |
| Design engineer (if design authority) | Fit, form, function impact for use-as-is / repair |
| Customer representative | Concession approval when required |

Decide within a set time (e.g. 2 working days) so suspect material does not pile up. Record each member's role,
not only signatures.

## Concessions and deviations

| Term | Timing | Example |
|------|--------|---------|
| Concession (waiver) | After manufacture: accept specific nonconforming items | 40 parts with bore 12.020 accepted by customer |
| Deviation permit | Before manufacture: produce differently for a limited quantity or time | Use alternative material for 2 lots |

A concession states: part, quantity, serials/lots, nonconformity, justification, limits, expiry, approver, and
how the items are identified when shipped.

## Scrap control

Scrap must not re-enter the process: mutilate safety parts, use locked red bins, reconcile scrap quantities with
NCRs, and record scrap cost in the NCR for cost-of-poor-quality reporting.
