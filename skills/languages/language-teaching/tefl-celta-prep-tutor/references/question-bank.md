# CELTA / TESOL Preparation Question Bank

Original practice questions for pre-course tasks and course input, with explanations. `scripts/quiz.py` reads
this file: keep the `### <id> | <domain>` heading, options `- A)`..`- D)`, `Answer:` and `Why:` lines.
Domains: Language, Phonology, Methodology, Planning, Classroom, Skills.

### L01 | Language
In "She has lived in Rome for ten years", which tense and aspect is used?
- A) Past simple
- B) Present perfect simple
- C) Present perfect continuous
- D) Past perfect simple
Answer: B
Why: has + past participle = present perfect simple; "for ten years" + still true now is a typical use.

### L02 | Language
Which sentence contains a modal verb of deduction?
- A) You must wear a seatbelt.
- B) She must be at home - her car is outside.
- C) I must call my mother.
- D) We must not smoke here.
Answer: B
Why: "She must be at home" draws a logical conclusion from evidence; the others express obligation or prohibition.

### L03 | Language
"Give up" in "He gave up smoking" is best described as:
- A) a prepositional verb
- B) a phrasal verb with an idiomatic meaning
- C) a collocation of two nouns
- D) a verb + adverb of manner
Answer: B
Why: Verb + particle with a meaning not predictable from the parts (stop) = phrasal verb.

### L04 | Language
Which is a good concept-checking question for "I used to play tennis"?
- A) Did you use to play tennis?
- B) Do you understand "used to"?
- C) Do I play tennis now?
- D) What is the past of "use"?
Answer: C
Why: CCQs check meaning without repeating the target language; "no longer true" is a key part of the concept.

### L05 | Language
In "The letter was sent yesterday", the verb form is:
- A) active past simple
- B) passive past simple
- C) passive present perfect
- D) past continuous
Answer: B
Why: was + past participle = past simple passive.

### L06 | Language
Which word is uncountable in standard English?
- A) suggestion
- B) advice
- C) chair
- D) problem
Answer: B
Why: "advice" takes no plural and no "an"; we say "a piece of advice".

### L07 | Language
"If I had known, I would have come" is an example of:
- A) first conditional
- B) second conditional
- C) third conditional
- D) zero conditional
Answer: C
Why: if + past perfect, would have + past participle: an unreal past condition.

### P01 | Phonology
How many phonemes are there in the word "through"?
- A) 2
- B) 3
- C) 5
- D) 7
Answer: B
Why: /θruː/ = /θ/ /r/ /uː/: three phonemes, though seven letters.

### P02 | Phonology
Which word has the stress on the second syllable?
- A) photograph
- B) photography
- C) photographic
- D) table
Answer: B
Why: pho-TO-gra-phy; PHO-to-graph, pho-to-GRA-phic, TA-ble.

### P03 | Phonology
The vowel sound in the unstressed syllables of "banana" (first and last) is:
- A) /æ/
- B) /ɑː/
- C) /ə/ (schwa)
- D) /eɪ/
Answer: C
Why: Unstressed vowels in English often reduce to schwa: /bəˈnɑːnə/.

### P04 | Phonology
The past tense ending in "wanted" is pronounced:
- A) /t/
- B) /d/
- C) /ɪd/
- D) silent
Answer: C
Why: After /t/ or /d/ the -ed ending adds a syllable /ɪd/.

### P05 | Phonology
Which pair is a minimal pair?
- A) ship / sheep
- B) cat / dog
- C) though / through
- D) read / red (spelling only)
Answer: A
Why: The words differ in one phoneme only (/ɪ/ vs /iː/).

### P06 | Phonology
In connected speech "next day" often sounds like /neks deɪ/. This is:
- A) assimilation
- B) elision
- C) intrusion
- D) linking r
Answer: B
Why: A sound (/t/) is dropped: elision.

### M01 | Methodology
Which is the correct order of stages in a PPP lesson?
- A) Practice, presentation, production
- B) Presentation, production, practice
- C) Presentation, practice, production
- D) Production, presentation, practice
Answer: C
Why: Present in context, practise with control, then produce more freely.

### M02 | Methodology
In a test-teach-test lesson, the first "test" is used to:
- A) give learners a grade
- B) find out what learners already know and need
- C) practise the target language freely after teaching
- D) check homework
Answer: B
Why: The first task diagnoses gaps; the teaching stage targets them; the second task checks progress.

### M03 | Methodology
Which activity is the best example of controlled practice?
- A) A class debate
- B) A role play with no script
- C) A gap-fill with one correct answer per gap
- D) Writing a story
Answer: C
Why: Controlled practice limits the language so learners focus on accuracy of the form.

### M04 | Methodology
Guided discovery means:
- A) the teacher explains the rule first
- B) learners work out the rule from examples with the help of questions
- C) learners read a grammar book alone
- D) translating the rule into L1
Answer: B
Why: Learners notice and infer the rule, guided by tasks or questions.

### M05 | Methodology
In task-based learning, the language focus usually comes:
- A) before the task
- B) instead of the task
- C) after the task cycle
- D) only in the next lesson
Answer: C
Why: Learners first use their own resources; language focus follows, based on the task.

### M06 | Methodology
Which is a recast?
- A) "Not go - went."
- B) "Yesterday you...?"
- C) "Oh, you went to the cinema?"
- D) "Past or present?"
Answer: C
Why: A recast reformulates the learner's sentence correctly without saying there was an error.

### PL01 | Planning
Which is the best main aim for a lesson?
- A) To teach the present perfect
- B) Students will understand the present perfect
- C) By the end of the lesson, learners will be able to ask and answer about life experiences using present perfect with ever/never
- D) Present perfect worksheet
Answer: C
Why: Aims are learner outcomes that are specific and observable.

### PL02 | Planning
"Anticipated problems" in a lesson plan should include:
- A) only problems with the projector
- B) problems with meaning, form, pronunciation and tasks, with solutions
- C) a list of difficult students
- D) nothing; problems cannot be anticipated
Answer: B
Why: Trainers expect language and task problems with concrete solutions.

### PL03 | Planning
A "stage aim" describes:
- A) the purpose of one stage of the lesson
- B) the main aim of the whole course
- C) the materials used
- D) the time of the lesson
Answer: A
Why: Each stage has its own aim that shows why it is there.

### PL04 | Planning
Why do teachers pre-teach a limited number of words before a reading task?
- A) To test vocabulary
- B) To remove words that would block the comprehension tasks
- C) To teach all new words in the text
- D) To fill time
Answer: B
Why: Only items that block the tasks are pre-taught; others can be guessed or ignored.

### PL05 | Planning
Which is a "subsidiary aim" for a speaking lesson about holidays?
- A) Learners will practise speaking fluently
- B) Learners will review past simple irregular verbs needed for the speaking task
- C) The teacher will reduce talking time
- D) To finish the coursebook unit
Answer: B
Why: Subsidiary aims support the main aim; "the teacher will reduce talking time" is a personal (teacher) aim.

### C01 | Classroom
What is the main purpose of instruction-checking questions (ICQs)?
- A) To check learners understand the grammar
- B) To check learners know what to do in the task before they start
- C) To test vocabulary
- D) To fill time while handing out papers
Answer: B
Why: ICQs check task instructions ("Do you write or speak?" "How long?").

### C02 | Classroom
When should materials usually be handed out for a pair task?
- A) Before giving instructions, so learners can read them
- B) After instructions and ICQs, so learners listen first
- C) Never; read the task aloud
- D) After the task
Answer: B
Why: Handing out first means learners read instead of listening to instructions.

### C03 | Classroom
Which is the best way to reduce teacher talking time (TTT)?
- A) Never speak in class
- B) Elicit, use pair work, and give short instructions with demonstrations
- C) Play more audio
- D) Ask only yes/no questions
Answer: B
Why: Eliciting and pair work increase learner talk; short instructions cut unnecessary teacher talk.

### C04 | Classroom
During a fluency activity, the teacher should usually:
- A) correct every error immediately
- B) monitor, note errors and give delayed feedback
- C) leave the room
- D) take part in every group's conversation
Answer: B
Why: Interrupting fluency work reduces practice; delayed correction keeps both fluency and accuracy.

### C05 | Classroom
"Nominating" means:
- A) choosing a specific learner to answer
- B) writing names on the board
- C) choosing a class monitor
- D) marking the register
Answer: A
Why: Nominating spreads participation and checks individuals, unlike open questions to the whole class.

### C06 | Classroom
The best seating for a mingle activity is:
- A) fixed rows
- B) learners standing and moving freely with space cleared
- C) a horseshoe with the teacher in the middle
- D) individual desks facing the wall
Answer: B
Why: A mingle needs learners to move and talk to many partners.

### S01 | Skills
A "gist" reading task asks learners to:
- A) find specific numbers
- B) understand the general idea quickly
- C) translate the text
- D) learn every new word
Answer: B
Why: Gist is the overall meaning; it comes before detail tasks.

### S02 | Skills
Scanning is reading to:
- A) find specific information quickly
- B) understand the writer's attitude
- C) check grammar
- D) read aloud
Answer: A
Why: Scanning = searching for specific items (names, dates, prices).

### S03 | Skills
In a listening lesson, learners should usually see the task:
- A) after listening
- B) before listening
- C) never
- D) only on the third play
Answer: B
Why: Setting the task first gives a purpose for listening.

### S04 | Skills
Why do learners compare answers in pairs after a listening task?
- A) To copy answers
- B) To check, build confidence and show the teacher whether another play is needed
- C) To finish quickly
- D) It has no purpose
Answer: B
Why: Pair checks lower anxiety and give the teacher information.

### S05 | Skills
In a productive writing lesson, a model text is used to:
- A) test reading
- B) show the genre, organisation and useful language before learners write
- C) be copied by learners
- D) fill time
Answer: B
Why: Analysing a model helps learners notice features they then use.

### S06 | Skills
Which is a sub-skill of speaking?
- A) Skimming
- B) Turn-taking
- C) Scanning
- D) Inferring meaning from text
Answer: B
Why: Turn-taking is part of interactive speaking; the others are reading sub-skills.
